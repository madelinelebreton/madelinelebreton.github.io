During my engineering internship at Casa RIA in Santiago de Compostela, I built three tools for architects and exhibition planning:

- [An accessibility mapping app](/work/isochrone-accessibility/) in R (Shiny, Leaflet) that maps walking, cycling and driving isochrones and nearby amenities from OpenRouteService and OpenStreetMap data for architects without GIS training, with QGIS-ready export.
- [A climate data pipeline](/work/aemet-climate-app/) in R and Python for AEMET climate records, with trend plots, Excel export and a user application.
- [A Kumu systems map of Galicia's water governance](/work/galicia-water-map/), developed from a literature review as a decision-support tool for exhibition planning.

Alongside the internship I took part in the Yale University summer program, which investigated ecosocial issues in the Fragas do Eume Natural Park.
