Sailed from Collingwood to Kincardine with Toronto Brigantine, working on teams of 7 to man the ship under way on the Great Lakes.

Climbed the mast to furl sails, learned nautical navigation with a chart, compass and triangulation, and took the helm during overnight watches. Passed the officer exam to become a certified Lead Hand.
