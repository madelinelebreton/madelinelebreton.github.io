From May to August 2026 I was an engineering intern at Fundación RIA, a nonprofit think tank that contributes to research, analysis, and territorial planning in Galicia. Working at Casa RIA in Santiago de Compostela, I built three tools for the architects and exhibition planners:

- [A Shiny app in R](/work/isochrone-accessibility/) that draws walking, cycling and driving travel time areas and nearby amenities from OpenRouteService and OpenStreetMap data. It is built for architects without GIS training and exports a QGIS ready file.
- [A climate data pipeline](/work/aemet-climate-app/) in R and Python that matches any list of Galician municipalities to the nearest AEMET weather station, pulls monthly records and exports Excel workbooks, plus a notebook of long-term temperature and rainfall trends.
- [A Kumu systems map](/work/galicia-water-map/) of how human activity and the water cycle affect each other in Galicia, built from a literature review with Pablo Gallego Picard. Kumu featured it as a Project of the Week.

Alongside the internship I took part in the Yale School of Architecture summer program, which investigated ecosocial issues in the Fragas do Eume Natural Park. I participated in field visits; identifying stakeholder needs and contributing to strategy development.
