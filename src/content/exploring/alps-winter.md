Explored the Alpine region in winter, hiking around Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte, in the Vercors and up Le Moucherotte.

Cross-country and downhill skiing, and went snowshoeing (raquettes) with the association [Alpes-La!](https://alpes-la.org/).
